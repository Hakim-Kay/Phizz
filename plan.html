Grand Opening plan

A free preview of how we'd run Phizz's first TikTok live.

  • When: Monday 16 November, 12:00 to 18:00
  • Where: Phizz office, Liverpool Street, London

You told us this is your first live, and that more lives depend on how it goes. So the plan does two jobs: sell well on 16 November, and show you clearly whether TikTok deserves more of your time.

Beat £10,000 on the day.

Emma at TikTok told us health brands usually make about £10,000 on their grand opening day, with around half from the live. That's the bar. We're planning to clear it.

  • £10,000+across the day
  • £5,000+from the six hours live

Afterwards, you'll know what sold, when and why, so deciding on more lives is easy either way.

Busy adults. One easy habit.

Your website speaks to four kinds of people, and your reviews show the same people buying:

  • Working parents
  • Commuters who train before work
  • Nurses on long shifts
  • Frequent travellers

Three things that shape the live

Their moments
The school run, a workout before work, a long shift, a flight. We sell Phizz in the moments your own site names, not as a list of ingredients.
Their questions
Taste and sugar come up most in your reviews. We mix one on camera, react honestly, and answer sugar with your own line: less sugar than a single grape.
Their season
Rivals sell summer, heat and festivals. Your live lands as party season and Christmas shopping begin, and none of them speaks to either yet.

The space is open. Big names like Berocca and Dioralyte have no UK TikTok account we could find, and the biggest UK hydration account we found has 22,300 followers.

As of October 2026.

Low prices are everywhere. This offer isn't.

You're planning your lowest price anywhere for the day. On its own, that won't stand out:

  • In Black Friday season, every brand has a low price.
  • Your website already gives free delivery over £15 and a free tube at £19.99.

So we add extras that cost you little, feel like a lot, and only the live has.

Three ideas to stop the scroll.

First thoughts, ahead of seeing your brand personas. We'll shape them with your team.

The fizz is the clock.

Every flash deal starts with a tablet dropped into a glass. When the fizz stops, the deal's gone. A countdown viewers can see and hear.

From the skies to your feed.

Phizz flies with Emirates. We tell that story on air, then hand viewers the same tablets in a TikTok-only bundle.

Open the office.

A quiet Monday at Liverpool Street becomes a behind-the-scenes tour. Anyone from your team who fancies it drops in for five minutes. Nobody has to, but viewers love meeting the people behind a brand.
